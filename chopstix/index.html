How do I answer?

Tap or click the keys on the screen.

Computer keyboard: the letters A S D F G H J K are white keys starting at C, and W E T Y U are the black keys. Pick “Computer keys” under Key labels to see them on the piano.

Your real piano: press “Listen to my piano” and allow the microphone. Play one note at a time for best results. Chords work best if you play the notes one after another.

MIDI keyboard: plug it in by USB (Chrome or Edge) and just play.
